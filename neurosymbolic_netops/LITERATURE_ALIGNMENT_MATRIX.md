# Literature Alignment Matrix

This matrix maps each theme of *Autonomous Fault Diagnosis and Closed-Loop Remediation in Telecommunication and Data Center Networks: A Critical Survey* to the implementation.

The project **does not reproduce** any of the surveyed systems. It implements the architectural ideas listed below, at testbed scale.

Status values: **IMPLEMENTED** · **PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED** · **SIMULATED** · **PLANNED** · **NOT IMPLEMENTED**.
